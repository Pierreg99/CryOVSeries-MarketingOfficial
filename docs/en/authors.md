# CryoSys for authors and ghostwriters

Enterprise v3. Public edition, 2026-10-09.

## The book does not die on the idea. It dies between chapter 4 and the voice.

A prompt can open a book. A manuscript needs the same through-line in chapter 18 that it had in chapter 2. Single-chat tools lose it there: repeated openings, invented citations, a voice that sounds like the model, chapters that ignore the brief.

Sixty-one percent of writers already use AI for support, mostly research, titles, and brainstorming. Only 7 percent use it to generate the content. Undisclosed generation for clients is treated as unethical in the cited industry line. Demand for human ghostwriters rose again once one-prompt books showed their limits. Source: Association of Ghostwriters, 2025 Industry Report, see `evidence/source-register.md` S1.

CryoSys for authors is not a ghostwriter replacement and not a book mill. It is a working atelier: research, structure, voice bible, consistency, fact-check, and sign-off. The human is the last authority.

## Who it is for

- Novels, memoir, nonfiction, practical guides, company histories, series.
- Ghostwriters who must hold several client voices and be able to disclose every use of AI.
- Authors who have the material but no reliable stages.
- Projects near a publisher, where undisclosed generation is a reason to reject.

Not for anyone who wants a finished book from one sentence, or who wants to hand authorship to a model.

## What the market has already shown

Ghostwriters who use AI use it for transcription, research, and titles, and they check every claim. Eleven percent call themselves advanced; 68 percent use it at least sometimes. They do not hand over the writing. Source: ASJA, S2.

Long drafts without integration lose chapters, references, and the through-line. Review can take longer than a clean draft. Source: Futurebraining, one practitioner report, S3.

Platform data, not an independent census: about 18 percent of books started in that app reached a manuscript. Formulaic openings and word-count drift appear in their tags. Source: AIWriteBook, S4.

German manuscript practice uses the same order: structure, then language, then the line. Source: scribigo, S6. Hallucinated citations in nonfiction are documented as a practitioner case, not as a rate. Source: Sowada, S7.

A Cambridge survey of UK novelists, not a world sample: 51 percent feared replacement of their fiction work, 39 percent reported income already reduced, 97 percent were extremely negative about AI writing entire novels. Source: S5. Welt describes the trust break as an autographic pact between the name on the cover and the reader. Source: S9.

## What a session does

1. Fix the brief. Genre, promise to the reader, target length, taboos, what must be disclosed.
2. Voice bible. Real samples, sentence length, favored words, word-jail, address. Later passages are held against it.
3. Matter map. Thesis or dramatic question, register, open sources, contradictions. Nothing is a fact without a source.
4. Scaffold before prose. A chapter contract: function, entry, exit, what this chapter must not repeat.
5. Research with a counter-check. Summaries are working material. Quotes, figures, and historical claims stay marked until a human clears them.
6. Write in stages. Proposal, diff, accept or reject. Word count is a contract.
7. Cross-check. Repeated openings, drifting names, broken through-lines, tone shift.
8. Sign-off log. What the human wrote, what was assisted, what was only structure or research.

Quality bar on this page: Q 0.92, PIS 0.84, on the Enterprise floor of Q at least 0.90 and PIS at least 0.82. Below the line, the work is revised, not published. SafeMode Level 2 stays on: no silent generation in the author's name.

## What you keep

- The voice. The model proposes; the bible decides.
- The facts. Unverified lines stay visible.
- Labeling. CryoSys does not issue ownership or publisher protection. It makes the assisted share documentable.
- Speed where it is expensive: research compression, outline, consistency across long form. Not the poetic choice in sentence 4.

## What this page does not claim

No bestseller. No publisher acceptance. No substitute for an editor, a lawyer, or a ghostwriting contract. No undetectable text. No prices: Enterprise v3 is price on request, Community v1 is the free learning line. No invented testimonials.

The line, if it is true: CryoSys holds the book together. The name on the cover writes it.
