# Community v1 and Enterprise v3

One catalogue, two editions. Do not mix the gates.

| | Community v1 | Enterprise v3 |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Free learning line, one task, a first checklist | Production line for a real manuscript or client book |
| Price | 0 | Price on request, until the maintainer publishes a number |
| Gate | Learning bar | Q at least 0.90, PIS at least 0.82, SafeMode Level 2 |
| Authors page bar in this repo | Not used | Q 0.92, PIS 0.84 |
| What it is | A way to learn the atelier | Research, voice bible, chapter contracts, fact marks, sign-off log |
| What it is not | A finished book | A book mill, a silent ghostwriter, a publisher acceptance guarantee |

Audience pack in this repository: Authors, German and English. Coaches, developers, and researchers are not claimed here.

No seat count, no SLA, no euro amount. Those stay unpublished until set in a later public commit.
