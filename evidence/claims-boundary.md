# Claims boundary

Public copy may say only what this file allows.

## Allowed

- CryoSys for authors is an atelier for research, structure, voice bible, consistency, and human sign-off.
- The human remains the author. The name on the cover writes the book.
- Assisted share can be logged: human-written, assisted, structure-only, research-only.
- Unverified claims stay marked until a human clears them.
- Enterprise v3 uses a production gate: Q at least 0.90, PIS at least 0.82, SafeMode Level 2.
- This authors pack was written to Q 0.92 and PIS 0.84. That is a delivery bar, not an external audit.
- Community v1 is the free learning line. Enterprise v3 is price on request.
- Industry context may be cited only as in `evidence/source-register.md`.

## Forbidden

- Any euro amount, seat count, or SLA not published by the maintainer.
- Bestseller, ranking, or publisher-acceptance promises.
- "Undetectable", "not AI", or any claim that assisted text needs no disclosure.
- Invented testimonials, client names, or case metrics.
- Claiming CryoSys replaces an editor, a lawyer, or a ghostwriting contract.
- Pasting third-party articles in full.
- Treating the AIWriteBook 18 percent figure as an independent industry census. It is first-party platform data.
- Treating the Cambridge novelist survey as a world sample. It is a UK novelist survey.

## Disclosure stance

Aligned with the 2025 ghostwriting reports: assistance for research, titles, transcription, and structure is common; generating client text without disclosure is treated as unethical by the cited associations. This pack does not tell anyone to hide assistance.
